Account & Data Deletion

App: SpinVersus  ·  Developer: Nova Spin Studio  ·  Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your SpinVersus account and the data associated with it.

1. How to Request Deletion

Option A — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open SpinVersus and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom of the screen and confirm.

Option B — By email

Don't have access to the app anymore? Send a deletion request from the email address linked to your account to:

novaspinstudio.games@gmail.com

Include your username and, if possible, the email you used to sign in (Google, Apple, or Facebook). We'll confirm your identity before processing the request.

2. What Happens After You Request Deletion

Deletion is scheduled immediately and becomes permanent 90 days later. During this window you can cancel the request by signing back in and choosing "Reactivate my account" — after 90 days the deletion cannot be undone.

3. What Data Is Deleted

Data typeDeleted after 90 days?
Profile (username, avatar, language, country)Yes
Game history, scores, leaderboard entriesYes
Friends list and friend requestsYes
Device identifierYes
Push notification tokenYes
Premium / token balanceYes

4. What Data May Be Retained

Your account and all associated data — including your sign-in identity — are fully and permanently erased 90 days after your deletion request, with the following exceptions:

  • Purchase records — transaction history may be retained by Google Play, Apple App Store, and RevenueCat under their own retention policies (SpinVersus does not control these) for tax and fraud-prevention purposes.
  • Revenue records — our own records of purchase and rewarded-ad events (date, product or ad unit, amount, and an internal pseudonymous user ID) are kept for accounting purposes. They contain no name, email, profile or game data.
  • Advertising data — data collected by Google AdMob when you watched optional ads is handled under Google's privacy policy. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

No profile, game, social, or sign-in data remains in SpinVersus systems beyond the 90-day period.

5. Questions

Contact us at novaspinstudio.games@gmail.com for any question about this process or our Privacy Policy.
