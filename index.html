Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026  ·  App: SpinVersus  ·  Developer: Nova Spin Studio

SpinVersus, developed by Nova Spin Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from the provider.
  • Profile data: Username and avatar you choose during setup.
  • Game data: Scores, game history, and friends list stored to provide the app's features.
  • Purchase data: In-app purchase transactions processed by Google Play / Apple App Store / RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
  • Device identifier: A unique device ID used to link your account to your device.
  • Push notification token: Used to send you friend requests and game invitations. You can disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Advertising data: If you choose to watch an optional rewarded video, Google AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. We record the fact that you watched an ad (to apply the daily limit and grant your reward) and the ad revenue associated with the view.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide and improve the app's features (multiplayer, leaderboards, friends)
  • To process and restore in-app purchases
  • To sync your data across reinstalls
  • To validate game answers automatically
  • To show optional rewarded video ads and grant the corresponding in-game tokens

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase – database and authentication hosting (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat – in-app purchase management (privacy policy)
  • Google Sign-In – authentication (privacy policy)
  • Apple Sign-In – authentication (privacy policy)
  • Facebook Login – authentication (privacy policy)
  • Google AdMob – rewarded video ads and consent management (privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps)
  • OpenAI – automatic validation of game answers; only the submitted word, theme, letter and language are sent, never your identity (privacy policy)
  • Expo Push Notifications / Firebase Cloud Messaging – delivery of push notifications (Expo privacy policy, Firebase privacy)

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see section 7). Accounts scheduled for deletion are permanently removed after 90 days.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with the third-party services listed above, to operate the app and, for Google AdMob, to show the optional rewarded ads.

6. Advertising & Consent

Ads in SpinVersus are optional: a video is shown only when you tap a "Watch" button to earn a free token (up to 3 per day). No banner or forced ads are shown.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can choose non-personalised ads or decline.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app: Profile → Delete my account. This schedules permanent deletion of all your data within 90 days.

You may also request data deletion by emailing us at novaspinstudio.games@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SpinVersus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Ads shown to users are subject to Google AdMob's content policies and the consent choices described in section 6.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact
