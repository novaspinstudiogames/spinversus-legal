Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026  ·  App: SpinVersus

SpinVersus ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from the provider.
  • Profile data: Username and avatar you choose during setup.
  • Game data: Scores, game history, and friends list stored to provide the app's features.
  • Purchase data: In-app purchase transactions processed by Google Play / Apple App Store / RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
  • Device identifier: A unique device ID used to link your account to your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide and improve the app's features (multiplayer, leaderboards, friends)
  • To process and restore in-app purchases
  • To sync your data across reinstalls

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase – database and authentication hosting (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat – in-app purchase management (privacy policy)
  • Google Sign-In – authentication (privacy policy)
  • Apple Sign-In – authentication (privacy policy)
  • Facebook Login – authentication (privacy policy)

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see section 6). Accounts scheduled for deletion are permanently removed after 90 days.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with the third-party services listed above, strictly to operate the app.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app: Settings → Delete my account. This schedules permanent deletion of all your data within 90 days.

You may also request data deletion by emailing us at nbastienfr@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

SpinVersus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: nbastienfr@gmail.com
